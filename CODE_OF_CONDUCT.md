# Community Guidelines / 社区准则

本文档适用于本项目的所有参与者，包括维护者、贡献者和用户。

This document applies to all participants in this project, including maintainers, contributors, and users.

---

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

我们致力于让参与者免受骚扰，无论年龄、体型、残疾、种族、性别认同、经验水平、教育背景、职业、民族、个人外貌、种族、宗教、性取向等。

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, education, profession, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

---

## Our Standards / 我们的标准

**积极的 / Positive:**
- 使用尊重和包容的语言 / Use welcoming and inclusive language
- 尊重不同的观点和经验 / Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- 优雅地接受建设性批评 / Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- 关注对社区最有利的事情 / Focus on what is best for the community
- 对其他成员表示同理心 / Show empathy towards other community members

**消极的 / Negative:**
- 使用性化语言或图像 / Sexualized language or imagery
- 人身攻击或不友好的评论 / Personal attacks or unwelcome comments
- 公开或私下骚扰 / Public or private harassment
- 未经明确许可发布他人私人信息 / Publishing others' private information without permission
- 其他不合理、不专业或令人反感的行径 / Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

---

## Responsibilities / 责任

项目维护者负责解释什么是可接受的行为，并负责任地执行公正和一致的行为准则执法。

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

---

## Enforcement / 执行

违反行为准则的维护者或其他人员可能会被永久剔除出项目团队。

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

---

## 举报 / Reporting

如遇到违规行为，请联系项目维护者 HoweBai：howebai@example.com

To report violations, contact the project maintainer HoweBai: howebai@example.com
